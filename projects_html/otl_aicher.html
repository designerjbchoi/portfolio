Who is the father of the geometric man?

Beyond the Pictograms: A Deep Dive into Otl Aicher
We all recognize those iconic symbols, but how well do we know the visionary behind the Munich Olympics? I created this 7x9 volume as a heartfelt celebration of Otl Aicher, a true titan of German typography and design. After plunging into a sea of books and websites, I gathered the essence of his life's work into a single, definitive tribute. To keep the pages as crisp as Aicher’s legendary grids, I chose Univers for the text—because when honoring a legend, only Frutiger’s masterpiece will do. This book is my personal thank you to Aicher for proving that great design speaks volumes without words, even if I used plenty to share his story!
