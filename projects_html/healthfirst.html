Company Intro

A comprehensive redesign of the Healthfirst member portal, improving claim submission, coverage lookup, and care coordination for 1.5 million members across New York.

01 USER RESEARCH

Conducted 12 user interviews and surveyed 200+ current members to identify pain points. Key frustrations centred on claim submission, coverage lookup, and provider search.

02 COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

Benchmarked Healthfirst against six major health insurance platforms — UnitedHealthcare, Anthem, Cigna, Oscar, Aetna, and EmblemHealth — across navigation, visual clarity, mobile UX, and claim workflows.

03 INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Restructured the content hierarchy to eliminate dead-ends and reduce task completion time. Developed a revised site map and user flows aligned with members' mental models.

04 WIREFRAME

Created low-fidelity wireframes for all primary journeys — claim submission, coverage check, provider search, and appointment scheduling — prioritising clarity and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.

05 VISUAL DESIGN

Applied the Healthfirst brand identity with improved contrast ratios, type hierarchy, and interaction patterns across all core screens to build member trust and reduce friction.

06 STYLE GUIDE

Established a cohesive design system — colour palette, typography scale, iconography, spacing tokens, and a reusable component library — to ensure consistency and support future design iterations.

07 FINAL PROTOTYPE

High-fidelity interactive prototype validated through two rounds of usability testing, demonstrating the full member journey from onboarding through claim submission and status tracking.

08 REFLECTION

This project reinforced the importance of accessibility and trust in healthcare UX. Key learnings include the value of early usability testing and iterative feedback. Future work would include A/B testing primary flows and multilingual support for Healthfirst's diverse member base.
