PES: Visualizing the Rules of the Game
Developed by Konami for PS4, Xbox One, and PC, PES is a premier soccer simulation that demands split-second decision-making. To enhance this experience, I designed a suite of 20 custom icons representing essential soccer rules.
My goal was to translate complex match situations into instant visual cues. These icons allow players to grasp the referee’s call at a glance, ensuring that the only thing they have to focus on is the next goal—not the rulebook.
